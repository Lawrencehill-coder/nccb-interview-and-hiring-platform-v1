NCCB Hiring SOP

Master Guide
Hiring SOPStructured hiring, documented from day one.
01

Requisition & Candidate Intake

Confirm the role, hiring manager, location, source and basic candidate information before the first interview.

  • Create or verify the candidate tracking number.
  • Attach resume and signed application when available.
  • Select the correct hiring track so role-specific scoring is applied.
02

First Interview — Phone Screen

The initial screener uses the scripted phone questions and the 5-criterion weighted scoring matrix.

  • Ask the core questions consistently.
  • Document candidate answers and observed behaviors.
  • Complete independent DISC read after the call.
  • Sign and choose Advance, Hold, or Do Not Advance.
03

Second Interview — Hiring Manager

The manager reviews the locked prior stage, then performs role-specific calibration.

  • Score all 7 criteria from 1–4.
  • Mark disqualifying observations immediately.
  • Use the question bank and live role-play evidence.
  • Complete the 90-day survival factors and independent DISC profile.
04

Calibration & Escalation

Use the weighted score as the primary decision aid; disqualifiers and critical survival signals can override a numeric score.

  • Do not “average away” a documented disqualifier.
  • Explain any override of the recommended band.
  • Use a follow-up interview when evidence is incomplete.
05

Final Decision & Approval

The final approver reviews all stages and gives an independent manager score and decision.

  • Confirm the file is complete.
  • Document basis of review and comments.
  • Sign and date Hired, Declined, Hold, or Further Review.
06

Record Retention & Handoff

Save the completed candidate record, export/print when needed, and preserve the interview audit trail.

  • Use Search Records for later review.
  • Do not create duplicate candidate files for later stages.
  • Keep hiring notes job-related, factual and professional.

First Interview — Phone Screen

Stage 1
Phone ScreenA consistent first conversation creates a fair starting point.

Core Phone Questions

1. Introduction & Interest
Ask: “Can you tell me a little about yourself and what interested you in applying for this position?”
2. Call Greeting Scenario
Ask: “Imagine you're answering a call from a new client. How would you greet them?”
Listen for professional greeting, identification, clarity, warmth and readiness to assist.
3. Customer Service Scenario
Ask: “Customer service is very important in this role. Can you describe a time you helped someone or handled a difficult situation?”
